About LuxStay Hotels

Our Story

Founded in 2010, LuxStay Hotels began with a simple vision: to create extraordinary hotel experiences that blend luxury with authentic local culture. What started as a single boutique hotel in Paris has grown into a global collection of 50+ unique properties across 20 countries.

Our Mission

We believe travel should be transformative. Our mission is to provide not just accommodation, but memorable experiences that connect guests with the essence of each destination. Every LuxStay property is carefully curated to reflect its location while maintaining our signature standards of comfort and service.
